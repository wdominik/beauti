ModelScope skin retouching model

Beauti uses the retouching U-Net published by Alibaba Tongyi Lab (iic) on ModelScope
under the Apache License 2.0; see ModelScope-Apache-2.0.txt.
https://www.modelscope.cn/models/iic/cv_unet_skin_retouching_torch/

Changes made by Beauti: the PyTorch checkpoint is exported to ONNX (opset 17) with
fixed input shapes, selected outputs, basic ONNX optimizations, and zero-padding
no-ops removed. Beauti also creates FP16 and 384 px variants, and on macOS Core AI
assets whose decoder upsampling is rewritten as an equivalent matrix product. The two
architecture files in third_party/modelscope_unet/ are unchanged.

Acquisition and runtime SHA-256 identities are in models/sources.json and
models/manifest.json in the included source.
